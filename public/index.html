Tower RTS build __BUILD__

Build rooms in your tower. Producers fill up over time. Tap them to collect; untapped rooms only trickle in 40%.

Rooms need a floor or a room beneath them. Same-type rooms side by side merge (up to 3) and produce more.

The red edge on the right is your lane face: the side the enemy will hit (coming in M2).

  • Tap / click a room: select (and collect)
  • Drag: pan · Pinch / wheel: zoom
  • 1–4: pick a room · Esc / right-click: cancel
  • Space: pause · − / +: speed · Del: demolish · `: debug
