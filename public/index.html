Tower RTS build __BUILD__

Goal: destroy the enemy Core before yours falls. At 5:00 overtime starts and both Cores burn, so whoever has hurt the other more wins.

Build rooms in your tower. Producers fill up over time. Tap them to collect; untapped rooms only trickle in 40%. Rooms need a floor or a room beneath them. Same-type rooms side by side merge (up to 3) and produce more.

Barracks and Hangars train their unit on repeat while you can pay and have supply (Quarters raise it). Units march on their own. Pick or pause a room’s unit in its panel. Pause it to save up for rooms.

Attackers hit the lane-side column first. Ground units reach floors 1–2; flyers reach any floor. A destroyed room becomes rubble, which still holds up the floor above; rebuilding on it costs half.

Expedition: one tower in fogged country. A Barracks set to Deploy Left/Right sends its units out to explore; Home keeps them as a garrison. Recall and Send are in the tower summary (tap empty space). Tap the sector strip to look at a sector.

  • Tap / click a room: select (and collect)
  • Drag: pan · Pinch / wheel: zoom · ⌖ / F: tower → field → enemy
  • 1–6: pick a room · Esc / right-click: cancel
  • Army / Build (top bar, B): Build pauses all unit training so you can save for rooms
  • Space: pause · − / +: speed · Del: demolish · `: debug
